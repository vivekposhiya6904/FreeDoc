# FreeDoc — Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors, maintainers, and community members are committed to making participation in the FreeDoc project a respectful, welcoming, harassment-free, and productive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

---

## Expected Standards

We encourage behavior that contributes to a positive, collaborative environment:

- **Professional Courtesy & Respect:** Treat all collaborators with dignity, empathy, and professional courtesy.
- **Constructive Discussion:** Focus on technical merit, objective evidence, and the best interests of the project. Critique ideas and implementations respectfully, never individuals.
- **Welcoming Newcomers:** Help newcomers get oriented, answer questions constructively, and encourage new contributors.
- **Respecting User & Contributor Privacy:** Safeguard the personal data, identities, and privacy of users and contributors in all public communications and project artifacts.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are strictly unacceptable within the FreeDoc community:

- The use of sexualized language, imagery, or inappropriate humor.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment of any form.
- Publishing others' private information (such as physical or electronic addresses, phone numbers, or private documents) without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source setting.

---

## Enforcement & Reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any behavior that violates this Code of Conduct.

### Reporting Process
- A dedicated confidential reporting email address is currently being configured by the project maintainers.
- In the interim, if you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report the incident confidentially to the repository maintainers through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting feature (if enabled on the repository) or by contacting the primary repository maintainer through their public GitHub profile.
- All reports will be handled with discretion and reviewed fairly.
- Project maintainers retain the responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and pull requests that do not align with this Code of Conduct.
